### SECTION 14 — GLOSSARY OF KEY TERMS
* **Homeostasis:** The self-regulating physiological processes by which biological systems maintain internal stability while adjusting to changing conditions.
* **Erythropoiesis:** The physiological process of red blood cell production.
* **Hemoglobin:** A respiratory protein in red blood cells that transports oxygen.
* **Microcytic:** Abnormally small red blood cells (explicitly defined in text as "small").
* **Hypochromic:** Abnormally pale red blood cells due to decreased hemoglobin content (explicitly defined in text as "pale").
* **Plasma Albumin:** The most abundant circulating plasma protein produced by the liver, essential for regulating fluid balance and colloid osmotic pressure.
* **Hypoalbuminemia:** Abnormally low level of albumin in the blood plasma.
* **Edema / Peripheral Tissue Edema:** Swelling caused by abnormal accumulation of fluid in interstitial body tissues.
* **Thermoregulation:** The physiological mechanism by which internal body temperature is maintained within normal homeostatic limits.
