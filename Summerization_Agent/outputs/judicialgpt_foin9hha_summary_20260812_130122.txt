### SECTION 3 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This matter arises from Sessions Case No. 123 of 2022, stemming from FIR No. 145/2022 registered at P.S. Model Town, Lahore. On February 10, 2022, at approximately 9:00 p.m., near a mosque in Model Town, Lahore, the accused, Muhammad Ali, allegedly shot the deceased, Muhammad Riaz, in the head at close range with a pistol, resulting in his instant death. The crime was witnessed by PW-2 (Muhammad Imran) and PW-3 (Abdul Rehman), leading to an FIR lodged by the deceased’s brother (PW-1). The police recovered the murder weapon (pistol, Exh.P-1) via ASI Muhammad Ashraf (PW-5), and Dr. Amjad Ali (PW-4) performed the post-mortem examination, confirming a single gunshot entry wound to the head. 

The accused pleaded not guilty to the charge framed under Section 302(b) PPC on April 22, 2022. In his examination under Section 342 Cr.P.C., the accused claimed false implication and alibi, but produced no defense evidence. The Sessions Court, Lahore found the prosecution's eyewitness accounts consistent, credible, and corroborated by medical evidence. On August 20, 2022, the court convicted Muhammad Ali under Section 302(b) PPC, sentencing him to imprisonment for life and a fine of Rs. 50,000/- (in default, six months' simple imprisonment), extending the benefit of Section 382-B Cr.P.C. from February 10, 2022.
