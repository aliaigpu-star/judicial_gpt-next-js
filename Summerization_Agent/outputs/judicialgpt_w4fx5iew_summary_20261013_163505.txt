### SECTION 14 — GLOSSARY OF KEY TERMS
* **AIoT (Artificial Intelligence of Things):** The integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies with Internet of Things (IoT) infrastructure to achieve efficient IoT operations and data analysis.
* **Edge AI / Edge Computing:** Processing data locally on physical devices or local nodes ("at the edge") rather than sending all data to a centralized cloud server, reducing latency.
* **ESP32:** A low-cost, low-power system-on-a-chip microcontroller series with integrated Wi-Fi and dual-mode Bluetooth.
* **LoRaWAN (Long Range Wide Area Network):** A low-power, wide-area networking protocol designed to wirelessly connect battery-operated 'things' to the internet in regional, national, or global networks.
* **MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport):** A lightweight, publish-subscribe network protocol that transports messages between devices.
* **CoAP (Constrained Application Protocol):** A specialized web transfer protocol for use with constrained nodes and constrained networks in IoT systems.
