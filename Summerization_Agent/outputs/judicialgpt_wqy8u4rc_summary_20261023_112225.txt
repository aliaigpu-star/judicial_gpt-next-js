### SECTION 14 — GLOSSARY OF KEY TERMS
* **CLO (Course Learning Outcome):** An educational benchmark defining the knowledge/skills students should demonstrate (e.g., explaining homeostasis and physiological regulation).
* **Erythropoiesis:** The physiological process by which red blood cells (erythrocytes) are produced.
* **Hemoglobin:** A iron-rich respiratory protein in red blood cells responsible for transporting oxygen.
* **Microcytic:** Unusually small red blood cells.
* **Hypochromic:** Red blood cells that are paler than normal due to insufficient hemoglobin.
* **Albumin:** The primary and most abundant plasma protein synthesized by the liver, essential for maintaining oncotic/colloid osmotic pressure and fluid balance in the cardiovascular system.
* **Hypoalbuminemia:** An abnormally low level of albumin in the blood plasma.
* **Peripheral Tissue Edema:** Swelling caused by excess fluid accumulation in tissues outside the central circulation, often caused by altered colloid osmotic pressure.
