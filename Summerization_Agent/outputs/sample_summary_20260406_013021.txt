### SECTION 3 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY  
The Supreme Court of Pakistan heard civil petitions regarding the implementation of judgments/orders related to the grant of "generation allowance" to certain employees. The petitioners, representing Wapda, sought relief against an order dated 17.07.2017 passed by the Federal Service Tribunal, Islamabad. The Court considered the repeated failure of the State functionaries to implement judgments and the legal issues surrounding the grant of generation allowance. The Court expressed disappointment and shock at the conduct of the State functionaries but opted for judicial restraint. The Court directed the implementation of its orders and imposed a special cost on the Chairman, Wapda.

---

### SECTION 4 — DETAILED FACTUAL BACKGROUND
The petitioners, Wapda through its Chairman and others, filed civil petitions (No.3451 to 3464 of 2017 and No.3473 to 3651 of 2017) against an order dated 17.07.2017 of the Federal Service Tribunal, Islamabad. The order allowed Miscellaneous Petitions filed by the respondents, seeking implementation of judgments/orders of the Service Tribunal. The petitioners are functionaries of the State, and the respondents are Raja Iftikhar Ahmed and others. The case involves the grant of "generation allowance" to certain employees of Tarbela Power Plant, which was allowed to generation staff but denied to other employees. The employees filed representations before the competent authority, which were dismissed. They then approached the Service Tribunal, which allowed their appeals on 22.03.1997. The petitioners challenged this order before the Court through Civil Petitions No.840 to 844 of 1997, which were dismissed on 10.10.1997.

### SECTION 5 — LEGAL ISSUES FRAMED
1. Whether the Service Tribunal's order dated 17.07.2017 is legally and factually tenable.
2. Whether the petitioners' failure to implement the judgments/orders of the Service Tribunal and the Court constitutes a violation of the rule of law and the Constitution.
3. Whether the grant of generation allowance to certain employees is lawful and in line with the policy of Wapda.
